Privacy Policy for the app “Punktgar”

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app dev.haviapps.ovenplanner · Deutsche Fassung

In short: The app collects no personal data, sends nothing to any server and has no internet access. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Controller

Havraz Othman
Email: havio.apps@gmail.com

2. What data the app processes

The app only processes what you enter yourself: names of dishes and steps, instructions for the steps, times, and the equipment of your kitchen. This is stored only locally in the app's private storage (file plan.json).

Other apps cannot read this storage. When you uninstall the app, Android deletes it completely. There is no cloud backup.

3. What the app does not do

  • No internet connection – the app does not have the INTERNET permission.
  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no registration.
  • No advertising, no analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No sharing of data with third parties – no data is transmitted.
  • No location data, no contacts, no access to photos or files.

“Share app” in the menu opens your phone's share menu with a sentence and a link to Google Play. You choose where it goes. The app itself sends nothing.

4. Permissions and what they are for

PermissionWhat forData?
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Setting reminders at the right moment. You grant it yourself; without it the app remains fully usable, just without reminders. no
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Showing the reminder. Requested when you set alarms, and can be declined. no
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restoring reminders after the device restarts – Android discards them on startup. no
FOREGROUND_SERVICE While reminders are set, the app shows a persistent notification with the countdown to the next task. It disappears when the reminders are cleared or the last step is reached. no
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE From Android 14, such a service must state its purpose. This permission is that statement – it unlocks no additional function. no

None of these permissions collects, transmits or stores personal data.

5. Legal basis

Since the controller does not process any personal data within the meaning of Art. 4(1) GDPR, no legal basis under Art. 6 GDPR is required. The entries stored locally on your device are under your sole control.

6. Your rights

You have the rights under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR – access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. Since we store no data about you, we cannot hand over or delete anything. You delete your entries yourself by uninstalling the app or removing its data in the Android settings.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

7. Obtaining the app through Google Play

The app is obtained through Google Play. Google processes data under its own responsibility, for example about your account and the download. We have no influence on this. The Google Privacy Policy applies.

8. Changes

If the app changes so that this policy no longer applies, it will be updated beforehand. The date above shows the current version.
